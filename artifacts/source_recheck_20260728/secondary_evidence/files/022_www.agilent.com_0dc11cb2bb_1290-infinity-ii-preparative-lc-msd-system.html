    Automated high-throughput purification chromatography

    1290 Infinity II Preparative LC/MSD System

    The 1290 Infinity II Preparative LC/MSD System is designed for high-throughput purification chromatography with enhanced precision, making it the ideal choice for generating high-quality compounds in biological screening and drug discovery. Offering an intuitive approach to mass confirmation, it simplifies mass-based analysis just as easily as UV detection, driving efficiency in your lab.

    This high-throughput purification system coupled with the InfinityLab Pro iQ Plus, the next-generation LC mass detector, ensures accurate mass-directed purification with a mass range of up to 3000 m/z, perfect for oligos and peptides. The Agilent 1290 Infinity II Preparative Open-Bed Sampler/Collector provides flexibility, adapting seamlessly to various applications and optimizing bench space. With mass-selective fraction triggering, only the most relevant fractions are collected, reducing reanalysis time and significantly improving productivity and purification quality.

    The 1290 Infinity II Preparative LC/MSD System delivers exceptional performance and flexibility in drug discovery or high-throughput applications while streamlining your LC/MS workflows with Agilent OpenLab CDS.

    • Features
    • Preparative LC/MSD System featuring fully automated sample injection and fraction collection. Maximize throughput, enhance purity, and reduce reanalysis time with mass-triggered purification with Pro iQ Plus (mass detector) for precise, efficient compound isolation.
    • Highly reliable and robust system designed to handle increasing workload demands with efficiency and precision. Ensures consistent performance, minimizing downtime while maximizing productivity for high-throughput purification chromatography and analysis.
    • Easy-to-change pump heads offer a dynamic flow range up to 200 mL/min at maximum 600 bar, providing versatility across a wide range of preparative workflows.
    • Lowest delay volume reduces peak dispersion and carry-over, ensuring the highest sample recovery and purity for precise and reliable results.
    • Upgradeable fraction capacity allows flexibility to expand based on your evolving demands.
    • Integrated automated fraction delay sensor technology enhances purity and recovery of collected fractions for more accurate results.
    • Seamless integration of all standard collection modes with compatibility across multiple trigger sources, ensuring effortless alignment with existing purification methods for enhanced flexibility and efficiency.
    • Enhanced control and organization with five multipurpose slots, optimizing the arrangement of valves, fraction delay coils, and the Agilent 1290 Infinity II MS Flow Modulator, resulting in a significant boost in productivity and operational efficiency.
    • Clean software infrastructure with Agilent OpenLab CDS, ensuring a familiar and reliable operation for seamless user experience.
    • Integrated leak sensor: Automatically shuts down the entire system upon detecting a leak, providing enhanced safety and preventing potential damage to the system or samples during operation.
