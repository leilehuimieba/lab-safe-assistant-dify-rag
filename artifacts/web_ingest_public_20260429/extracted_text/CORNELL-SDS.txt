## Safety Data Sheets (Chemwatch)

Safety Data Sheets and other chemical safety information are available on-line.

Cornell EHS provides campus wide access to Chemwatch, an online Safety Data Sheet (SDS) database consisting of over 250,000 Chemwatch authored documents, and 13 million vendor supplied documents.

Using this database, you can:

Search for Safety Data Sheets

Search for Safety Data Sheets

Download documents to your desktop computer

Generate and print chemical labels

Access the system from the desktop any iOS or Android Device

In addition to Chemwatch, the Cornell libraries web site provides access to Bretherick’s Handbook of Reactive Chemical Hazards from 2007, which is the latest edition. This reference provides known incompatibilities between specific combinations of chemicals.
