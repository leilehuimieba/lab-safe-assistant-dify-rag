Access control provisions (key switch and/or password 
protection feature) are standard features on most Esco 
cabinets.

Wearing a back-fastened lab coat (to protect the operator 
from splashes) as well as double gloving (over the cuffs) 
should be practiced.

DO NOT obstruct any of the air grilles in the front or back 
of the work zone. Work as deep into the work zone as 
possible. 

Plan your work before starting and place all items inside 
the cabinet before use (this avoids having to take your 
arms out from the work zone which disturbs the air 
barrier). Always work from “clean” to “dirty”, segregating 
contaminated and non-contaminated materials.
