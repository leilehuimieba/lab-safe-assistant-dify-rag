 SAFE HANDLING 

NEVER WORK ALONE

PPE: 

• Goggles & Faceshield 
• Utility neoprene or nitrile 
glove over exam-style 
nitrile glove 

• Lab coat 
• Chemical resistant apron 

Emergency Response 
RAPID AND IMMEDIATE SKIN & EYE DECONTAMINATION IS CRITICAL 

ALWAYS SEEK MEDICAL ATTENTION IMMEDIATELY AFTER AN EXPOSURE 

SKIN EXPOSURE 
• Remove contaminated clothing 
• Rinse area for 5 minutes using the 

emergency shower or a drench hose 
• Apply calcium gluconate gel to affected 
site, reapplying every 10-15 minutes 
until EMS arrives 

• Use emergency shower or drench hose 

for 15 minutes if no calcium gluconate is 
available 

EYE EXPOSURE 
• Immediately flush eyes in emergency 

eyewash for 15 minutes 

MEDICAL ATTENTION: 
• For all eye exposures and significant skin 
exposures - contact 911 immediately 
• All other exposures - follow up at YNHH 

emergency room after calcium gluconate 
treatment or emergency shower
