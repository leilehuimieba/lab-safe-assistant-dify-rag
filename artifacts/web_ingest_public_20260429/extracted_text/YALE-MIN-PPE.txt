When working in a 
laboratory where hazardous 
materials are used, wear:

• Safety glasses or

other eye protection

• Long pants
• Closed toe, solid top shoes

Minimum 
Laboratory 
PPE

When directly handling
hazardous materials, add:

• Safety glasses
• Lab coat
• Gloves, if touching

potentially contaminated or
hazardous materials

Travel safely on your path to discovery.
