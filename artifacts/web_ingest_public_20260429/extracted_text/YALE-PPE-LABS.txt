Biohazard experiments are classified based on risk. The starting point for risk assessment is the assignment of a biohazard to a specific Risk 
Group. There are 4 Risk Groups (RGs) based on risk to the individual and the community. RG1 is the lowest risk and RG4 is the highest. Risk 
Group assignments for human pathogens can be accessed at: http://www.absa.org/riskgroups/index.html 
• For other experiments, researchers can refer to the Gradations of Risk Table referenced in this document.
